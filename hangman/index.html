How to Play

A new word is picked every day. Guess its letters before the hangman is complete.

  • Draw a single letter on the chalk slate with your mouse or finger — upper or lowercase, whatever feels natural.
  • The board shows its best guess at what you drew. Not quite right? Tap one of the alternates, or clear the slate and try again.
  • Tap Confirm Guess to submit that letter.
  • Guess right and every matching letter is revealed. Guess wrong and the hangman gains a part — you get 6 wrong guesses.
  • Prefer typing? Use the "Or type:" box just below the slate label, then tap the ✓ next to it to guess that letter directly, no drawing required.
