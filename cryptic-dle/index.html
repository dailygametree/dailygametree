How to Play

Every day you get one real cryptic crossword clue — a straight definition plus a bit of wordplay (an anagram, a hidden word, a reversal, a charade, a double definition, or a homophone), all pointing at the same answer. The number in parentheses tells you how many letters it has.

Type a guess that length and press Enter. Unlike a typical word-guessing game, you get no letter-by-letter hints — a guess is only ever marked right or wrong as a whole, so the clue itself has to do all the work:

A muted row with an ✕ means that guess was wrong — but not which letters, so you can’t reverse-engineer the answer letter by letter. A green row with a ✓ means you solved it.

There’s no limit on guesses — keep going, each attempt just adds another row. The only things being timed are your guess count and how long you take.

A new Cryptic-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
