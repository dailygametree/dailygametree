How to Play

Fill every empty cell with a digit from 1–9 so that each digit appears exactly once in every row, every column, and every bold 3×3 box.

Tap a cell to select it, then tap a number below the board (or type it on your keyboard). Bold cells are your starting clues and can’t be changed.

Tap the pencil icon ✎ to switch to notes mode — taps then jot small candidate numbers into a cell instead of filling it in. A conflicting entry is highlighted in red so you can spot mistakes as you go.

A new Sudoku-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC. Clue count varies day to day.
