Tap a cell (or a clue in the lists below) to select it, then type a letter. Tap a cell a second time to switch between its across and down word. Use the arrows in the clue bar, or arrow keys, to move around. Wrong letters show up in amber so you can spot and fix them.

The bulb button 💡 reveals the selected cell’s letter if you get stuck. A new Crossword-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
