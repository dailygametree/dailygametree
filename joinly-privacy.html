Joinly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the Joinly puzzle app for Android ("the app") handles information. The app is made by dailygametree ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, please get in touch through our contact page.

The short version

  • Joinly has no accounts or sign-in, and we don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress, best times, streaks and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following on your phone: which levels you've solved and your best times, your daily puzzle results and streak, any puzzle you're part-way through, and your settings (such as whether sound effects are on). This information stays on your device. It is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data, and it can also be backed up by your phone's own Android backup if you use that feature.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Joinly is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location (derived from your IP address);
  • device information such as the model, operating system and language;
  • information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This information is collected and handled by Google under Google's own policies. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: when you first open the app, you'll be asked (using Google's consent tool) whether you agree to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • If you don't agree to personalised ads, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your interests.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't collect any ourselves. The only third party that receives information through the app is Google, for advertising as described above.

Children

Joinly is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, please contact us.

Security

The app doesn't send your game data anywhere. Advertising data is sent by Google's software using encrypted connections.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to access or delete it. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most requests are best directed to Google for advertising data (see the links above). You can delete everything the app stores on your device by uninstalling it or clearing its data in your phone's settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Please reach us through our contact page.

This policy applies to the Joinly app for Android. For our website, see the dailygametree.com privacy policy.
