How to Play

Every day you get a real dictionary definition — your job is to guess the word it defines. Every word is at least 5 letters.

Type a guess the same length as the answer and press Enter. Each letter is colored to show how close you are:

W
O
R
D

Green means that letter is correct and in the right spot. Yellow means it’s in the word but in the wrong spot. Gray means it’s not in the word at all.

There’s no limit on guesses — keep going, each attempt just adds another row. The only things being timed are your guess count and how long you take.

A new Define-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
