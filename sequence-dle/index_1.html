How to Play

Every day a new sequence of seven numbers appears, with a couple of them missing. Work out the pattern and type the missing numbers into the blank tiles.

  • Press Check to test your answers — correct ones lock in green, wrong ones shake so you can try again.
  • Unlimited guesses — take as long as you like.
  • Sequences can go up, down, or alternate — and can involve addition, multiplication, squares, cubes, primes and more.
  • Stuck? The 💡 hint reveals the type of pattern, without giving away any numbers.

A new sequence every day at midnight UTC.
