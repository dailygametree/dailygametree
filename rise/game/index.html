One balloon. One life. See how high you get.

How to Play

  • Steer — drag anywhere, or use ← → / A D
  • Climb — the balloon rises on its own, and the sky moves faster the higher you get
  • One hit pops it — so give hazards room
  • Helium canister — grab one for a few seconds of shield
  • Close calls — graze a hazard without touching it for bonus altitude
