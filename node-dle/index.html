How to Play

Every day's board is a fixed network of nodes with grey lines already drawn between them. Your job is to trace over every single line, turning it solid — without ever tracing the same line twice.

Press down on a node and drag your finger along a grey line, staying close to it, until you reach the node at its far end — a solid line follows your finger as you go. Each node gets numbered the first time you reach it (1, 2, 3…), so you can see the order you've traced things in. You can only start your next stroke from wherever the path currently ends — that node stays filled in, even if it's an earlier, lower number the path has looped back around to. You can't jump to a different, unconnected node.

Where you start matters — not every node leads to a full solve, and picking the wrong one can leave you stuck with connections you can no longer reach. The board won't let you needlessly strand a connection when a safer move is sitting right there, but it can't save you from a bad starting node. If you get stuck, Undo your way back and try again.

The puzzle is solved once every line is traced. It's always possible to do the whole board in a single unbroken stroke — that's a perfect solve. Every extra time you have to lift and restart counts against you.

A new Node-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC. Both the shape and the number of connections change daily.
