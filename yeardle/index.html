How to Play

Read the clue on the flashcard and guess the year the event happened. You have 5 tries. Each guess must be a 4-digit year — tap the number keys, then Enter.

After each guess, the tiles change colour to show how close you were:

0
8
3
1

Green — that digit is correct and in the right spot.
Red — that digit is wrong for that spot (even if it appears elsewhere in the year).

In the example above the year was 1932 — only the 3 landed in the right place.

Some very old events happened before the year 1000, so their year is padded with leading zeros — e.g. the year 9 CE is entered as 0009.

A new Yeardle unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
