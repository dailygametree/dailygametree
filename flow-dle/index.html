How to Play

Every colour has two dots on the board. Press and drag from one dot to draw a pipe across empty cells to its matching partner.

Pipes can't cross each other or double back on themselves — once a cell is claimed by a pipe, no other pipe can use it. Drag back over your own pipe to erase part of it, or press a dot again to clear that colour and start over.

The puzzle isn't solved just by connecting every colour — every single cell on the grid has to be covered by exactly one pipe, with no empty squares left over.

A new Flow-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC. The number of colours varies day to day.
