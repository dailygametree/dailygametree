How to Play

Every day has a target — a random whole number of seconds between 1 and 30, shown right on the page. That part isn’t hidden.

What’s hidden is the clock. Tap Start, wait a beat for “Go”, and the timer begins running silently — no digits, no progress bar, nothing counting up on screen. Tap Stop the instant you think today’s number of seconds has actually elapsed.

Once you stop, your real time is revealed alongside the target, so you can see exactly how early or late you were — down to the hundredth of a second.

You can try again as many times as you like each day — every attempt is logged, and your closest one is your best for the day. Nothing to solve, no wrong answers, just your internal clock versus the real one.

A new number unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
