How to Play

One hand of blackjack a day, dealt from a shuffle that's the same for everyone. You and the dealer each get two cards — one of the dealer's stays face down.

Hit takes another card. Stand ends your turn and lets the dealer play. Double Down takes exactly one more card and stands automatically — only available on your first decision.

The dealer stands on all 17s (soft or hard) and must otherwise hit to 17. A natural blackjack (an Ace with a 10-value card on your first two) wins immediately unless the dealer also has one, which pushes.

One hand per day — a new shuffle unlocks every day at midnight UTC.
