How to Play

Sixteen tiles, all face down. Tap one to flip it over, then tap a second one.

If the two shapes match — same icon and the same colour — they stay face up. If they don't match, both flip back down after a moment. Find all eight pairs to clear the board.

A new Match-dle unlocks every day at midnight UTC. The colours change every day, but it's always these four shapes.
