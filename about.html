About Us

DailyGameTree is a small, independent home for free daily browser puzzle games. It started in 2026 as a personal project: a love of the daily-puzzle format popularised by games like Wordle, and a want to build a few original spins on it rather than just play someone else's.

The idea behind the name is simple. Some visitors come back every day for the games they already know and love — staying rooted. Others are here to see what's new and try something they haven't played before — branching out. DailyGameTree is meant to hold room for both.

Right now the site is home to two games:

  • 67dle — a daily maths puzzle where you combine four numbers with arithmetic (and, in Hard mode, factorials and powers) to hit exactly 67.
  • Yeardle — a daily history puzzle where you read a short clue about a real historical event and guess the year it happened in, Wordle-style.

More games are planned. Each one is built from scratch, aims to be fair to solve, and — like the rest of the site — is free to play, with no account required.

DailyGameTree is currently a one-person effort, run in spare time rather than as a company. If you've got feedback, spotted a bug, have an idea for a puzzle, or just want to say hello, the Contact page is the best way to reach out.
