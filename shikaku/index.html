How to Play

Every clue number is the area (in cells) of the rectangle it sits inside. Click or tap a cell and drag to draw a rectangle.

A rectangle is valid when it contains exactly one clue, and its width × height equals that clue's number. Rectangles can't overlap.

Above: a 4×3 grid split into a 3×2 rectangle (6), a 1×2 rectangle (2), and a 4×1 rectangle (4) — every cell covered exactly once.

Tap any placed rectangle to remove it and try again. When every cell is covered, you've solved it.

A new Shikaku unlocks every day at midnight UTC. Grid size varies day to day.
